Can anything warn of a split between NIFTY and the US?

Not that we could find. The correlation line reacts late, and none of 70 tests of bond spreads and other measures gave an earlier warning. The best open idea is that India gains when AI and chip stocks fall. It is growing since 2023 but not proven.

The signal is there, and it is late

We simulated a link that drops suddenly from 0.30 to −0.20. A 13-week correlation takes about 36 trading days to show it. Faster versions take about 10, and raise about three false alarms a year.

This time the fast measure first went below zero on 15 October 2026 and reached −0.36 on 3 November. By 23 November it was back at 0.05. It crossed below zero 9 times in 2024 and 6 in 2025, so it cannot be used alone. Korea, Taiwan and Japan read 0.53 to 0.67 against the S&P on the same measure.

Dips below zero are common even when nothing has changed. With a constant link, simulations since 2023 produce about 2.5 separate dips, and 47% of them show three or more. We saw three.

This quarter, India broke away from Korea and Taiwan

From 1 October to 24 November 2026 NIFTY rose 12.1% while Korea fell 26.8%, Taiwan 16.3% and the Nasdaq 11.6%. Same-date correlations with NIFTY turned negative, and the 95% ranges stay below zero. The quarter was picked after seeing it.

Korea and Taiwan kept their usual link to the S&P (0.45 and 0.47). India is the market that separated.

Does decoupling mean money rotated?

Partly, for this quarter. Foreigners bought a net 87 thousand crore rupees of Indian shares, and in Q3 2025 they sold 77 thousand. The general rule does not hold.

The AI and chip idea

Each bar is how much NIFTY moved in the same week for each 1% chips gained over the S&P, after allowing for the S&P. Below zero means India gains when chips lag.

There was no effect in 2015 to 2022 (t = 0.6). It was −0.07 in 2023 to September 2026 (t = −1.9) and −0.09 in 2025 to September 2026 (t = −2.3). Our rule needed it in both long periods, so it fails. The last bar covers eight weeks (t = −1.8). Last week's chip move did not reliably predict NIFTY, so it gives no early warning.

In this quarter's five weeks of heavy chip underperformance NIFTY beat the S&P by 4.4% on average, mostly in the week ending 6 November.

The frozen test

We fixed this test on 25 November 2026 for the 26 weeks ending 4 December 2026 to 28 May 2027. It passes if the chip effect is below zero with one-sided p under 0.05. We look once, after it ends.

Where weekly data stands with November included

November belongs in the data. With it, the same-Friday weekly correlation is 0.44, 0.38, 0.32 and 0.03 for 2023 to 2026. The 2026 drop from the three earlier years has a one-sided p of 0.06, and the 2026 figure has a range of −0.26 to 0.54. India has diverged from the US in returns this quarter. A negative correlation is not established.

What to do

  • Watch the fast measure beside the 13-week line, and treat a dip as a reason to look. It is no trade signal.
  • Leave the chip test alone until it ends.
  • Find country ETF and fund flows for India, Korea, Taiwan and the US.
  • Stop testing yield spreads against this link.
Method and limits
  • Predictors use data before each Friday's close in India. Indian yields are lagged a full month and flows to the last complete month. Blocks do not overlap.
  • The check has 48 blocks of 4 weeks or 14 of 13 weeks, so modest effects could be missed.
  • Shared charts, the fourth quarter of 2026 and earlier yield tests had been seen before this protocol was written.
  • The delay simulation uses normal, independent days. Real breaks may look different.
  • Flow history is a third-party copy of NSDL data. The chip measure is SOXX against SPY.
  • A separate volatility project reserves January 2025 to October 2026, and these studies use that window.
